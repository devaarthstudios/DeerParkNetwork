Deer Park Network Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026 (v0.1.3)

Deer Park Network is a social app prototype by Devaarth Studios. This policy covers only the current prototype release (v0.1.3). It describes what this version does, not what future versions may do.

What the app stores

Your profile, posts, comments, blocks and reports are stored only on your device. The prototype has no accounts and no servers of ours, and the app does not send your posts, photos, profile or location to us or anyone else.

Location

If you tap Location while writing a post, the app reads your approximate (coarse) location once and saves it with that post on your device. This version asks only for approximate location, not precise or background location, and posting works without it.

Photos

Photos are attached one at a time through Android's system photo picker. This version does not request access to your gallery or media library.

Backups and device transfer

The app disables Android cloud backup for its data and configures exclusions for Android device-to-device transfers, so your profile, posts and photos are not intended to be included in either. How these settings are applied can vary by device maker and Android version, and your device manufacturer's own backup tools are outside the app's control. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, the data stored by the app is deleted.

What the app does not do

This version has no advertising, analytics or tracking code, and we do not sell personal data. It does not access your contacts, camera, microphone or notifications.

Information you send us

If you tap Report a safety concern in the About screen, the app opens a draft email in your email app. This version of the app itself sends nothing. If you choose to send it, or if you email us in any other way, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to respond to your message and to handle safety, privacy or legal matters, and we keep it only as long as needed for those purposes. Please do not include passwords, financial details, health information or other sensitive details in your message. Your email provider handles the message in transit.

Google Play and testers

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google processes information about installs and testing under its own terms and privacy policy, and we may see limited information through Google Play Console, such as install and crash statistics. If you join a test, we hold the email address you used to join so that Google can give you access.

Legal requests

We may disclose information we hold if the law requires it, for example to report child sexual abuse material to the authorities.

Who can use the app

Deer Park Network is for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13. Please do not send us personal information if you are under 13. For child safety, see our Child Safety Standards.

Your choices and rights

Posts, photos and your profile are on your device, and you can delete them or clear the app's data at any time. To ask about, correct or delete any information we hold about you, such as an email you sent us, contact us below.

Changes to this policy

Future versions may add features, such as accounts, online posting or new permissions. Before any feature collects or shares data in a way not described here, we will update this policy and explain the new use in the app. Where the law or Google Play requires your consent or a parent's consent, we will ask for it at that time. Your use of this version is not consent to any future collection. Each version shows its date at the top.
