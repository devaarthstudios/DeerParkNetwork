Deer Park Network Child Safety Standards

Last updated: November 25, 2026

These are the child safety standards for Deer Park Network, an app by Devaarth Studios.

Our standard

Deer Park Network does not allow child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). This includes child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion and trafficking of children. Nobody may use the app for any of these.

How the app works today

The current version is an offline prototype. It has no accounts and no servers, and this version of the app does not send or upload anything. Posts, comments and photos stay on the device of the person who made them, so the app does not currently carry content from one person to another.

Reporting and blocking

In the app, long-press any post to report it or block its author. Because the prototype has no server, a report or block is saved only on that device. It is not sent to Devaarth Studios and we do not receive it. To raise a child safety concern with us, open the app's About screen and tap "Report a safety concern". This opens a draft email to the contact below in your own email app. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and the app does not attach any posts, photos or location. You can also email the contact below directly.

What we do about concerns

If we learn of CSAM or other CSAE content connected to Deer Park Network, we will act in line with these standards and the law, including reporting to the relevant authorities where the law requires it.

Future versions

If a future version lets people share content with each other, we will update these standards, and add the reporting and moderation that requires, before that feature is released.
